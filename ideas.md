# Selected Direction: Orbiting Care Map

## Design Movement

**Neo-editorial celestial interface.** The landing page borrows the reference reel’s immersive black field, rotating star motifs, and kinetic typography, then converts those gestures into a trustworthy care-system metaphor. This is not “space technology”; it is a visual model for no mother being lost between a field visit and a follow-up.

## Core Principles

1. **Care is visible.** Every key interface element should communicate connection, acknowledgement, and continuity rather than automation for its own sake.
2. **Motion has meaning.** Orbiting is reserved for active care pathways; pulses indicate pending confirmation; short glides denote a logged handoff.
3. **Field reality remains foregrounded.** The digital aesthetic must be balanced by Kannada language, clear human names, concrete follow-up tasks, and direct safety language.
4. **Calm outranks spectacle.** Rich motion is used with ample negative space, accessible contrast, and deliberate pauses.

## Color Philosophy

The base is a dense **midnight charcoal**, reflecting the reference’s immersive frame and creating space for luminous operational signals. A warm **marigold-gold** becomes MaatruMitra’s unmistakable action color: it evokes a protective lamp and lets safety-sensitive attention read clearly without using alarm-red as the dominant emotion. Soft cloud white carries explanation, while an earthy pale clay supports field-work context. A small leaf-green signal identifies confirmed handoffs, not clinical outcomes.

## Layout Paradigm

The page is arranged as a **vertical care journey**, rather than a centered product grid. A living orbit occupies the hero’s right field while the story begins at the lower left. Each later section emerges as a waypoint on an asymmetrical timeline, with a thin “care thread” continuously stitching the page together. Editorial content stacks left; evidence, system cards, and interface previews interrupt from the right.

## Signature Elements

1. **The Care Orbit:** a revolving set of stars, task nodes, and thin orbital tracks around a central mother-profile signal.
2. **The Handwritten Kannada Note:** short voice-note text is styled as authentic field input that resolves into structured, audited data.
3. **The Care Thread:** a warm, low-opacity line that travels through the page and connects the user story, workflow, and safety commitments.

## Interaction Philosophy

Controls feel operational, not ornamental. Hovering a care node reveals its role; activating a primary CTA opens a compact product-preview panel instead of navigating away. The voice-note demo moves progressively from raw note to structured record so the user understands each human-review point. Wherever a workflow suggests escalation, an explicit “ANM confirms” state remains visible.

## Animation

The care orbit performs a slow 22-second linear rotation; child nodes counter-rotate, remaining readable. Wide stars drift at irregular, low-amplitude intervals. The hero information panel rises and settles with a 450ms custom ease-out, while individual workflow steps enter with 55ms staggering. Every looping effect is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Buttons compress slightly on press; task confirmations use a short transform-and-opacity transition, never a flashy or diagnostic-looking pulse.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** supplies a sober, compassionate editorial voice for the headline and pull quotes. **Manrope** handles interfaces, labels, and body copy with clean legibility. **Noto Sans Kannada** is used wherever Kannada appears, retaining dependable script rendering. Headlines use tight, deliberate tracking; operational labels are smaller, uppercase, and broadly letter-spaced to make hierarchy immediately scanable.

## Brand Essence

**MaatruMitra is the Kannada-first follow-up intelligence layer that helps ASHA and ANM teams keep every pregnancy visible, accountable, and human-led.**

**Personality:** Protective, clear-eyed, field-close.

## Brand Voice

The voice is concrete and respectful. Headlines name the human outcome; CTAs describe the next visible action. It avoids both clinical certainty and generic technology claims.

> “A 30-second voice note can keep the next visit in view.”

> “See the follow-up, then let the ANM confirm it.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is an **open orbital arc embracing a four-point care star**, with a small detached dot signifying a field worker’s signal reaching the network. The MaatruMitra wordmark should pair a compact serif ‘M’ with sharply spaced sans-serif letterforms, rather than default typography.

## Signature Brand Color

**Deep Marigold — #E9A830.** It functions as the remembered, protective signal across the application.
